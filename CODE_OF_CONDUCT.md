# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and give them the benefit of the
doubt you would want.

## What this means here

**Good:** asking what a decision was for before calling it wrong. Saying "I don't
understand this" instead of "this makes no sense." Reviewing the change rather than the
person who wrote it. Saying so plainly when you were mistaken.

**Not welcome:** harassment or insults, personal or political attacks, dismissing someone
based on who they are or how much they know, publishing anyone's private information, or
persistently disrupting discussion after being asked to stop.

Contributors come from different countries, time zones, and experience levels. Someone
asking a question you find obvious is not wasting your time.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this
project.

## Enforcement

Report by email to the address on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/wondping0), or privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/wondping0/wondev/security/advisories/new) if
you would rather it stayed off-platform.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on severity the response ranges from a private
word to a permanent ban. The maintainer decides, and will explain the reasoning to the
person reported.

If the problem is with the maintainer, report it to GitHub directly.
